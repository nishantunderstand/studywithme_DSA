Not the kind that breaks your confidence. The kind that shows you where you actually stand.

A lot of students think they are doing well because everything feels comfortable.

• The playlist makes sense.
• The notes look familiar.
• The concepts seem easy.

And then reality arrives.

You give your first contest and solve one question. You sit for an OA and realize you can't solve problems without hints.

You give a mock interview and suddenly forget concepts you thought you knew. You try explaining your project and struggle to answer basic follow-up questions.

It's uncomfortable. But it's also one of the fastest ways to improve.

Because for the first time, you're no longer measuring yourself against a playlist. You're measuring yourself against a standard.

That's why I always tell students to:
• Give contests regularly
• Participate in hackathons
• Take mock interviews
• Attempt assessments without looking at solutions
• Put yourself in situations where you might fail


**The goal is to find your gaps before an actual company finds them.**
