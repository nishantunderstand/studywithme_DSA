When I started solving DSA problems, I used to spend hours on a single question.  
Reading, re-reading… staring at the screen.  
And eventually—either overcomplicating it or giving up.  
  
That’s when I started following a simple rule that actually changed the game for me:  
The 20-minute rule ✨  (Mostly for medium level problems)  
  
Here’s how it works:  
➡️First 5–7 minutes → just read and break the problem down. No code. Just understand.  
➡️ Next 8–10 minutes → brainstorm possible approaches, compare time complexities, think out loud.  
➡️ Last few minutes → if I’m stuck, I peek at hints or the editorial, then re-attempt it again.  
  
Why it works:  
✅ It trains your brain to think fast under pressure (like in real interviews).  
✅ It teaches you to move on without losing confidence.  
✅ It builds problem intuition with time.  
  
I didn’t just get better at solving problems—  
I got faster, sharper, and more structured.  
  
Mock interviews felt less scary. Real interviews became manageable.  
Because honestly, most of it is time and clarity.  
  
All the best!❤️
