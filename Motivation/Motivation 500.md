Why solving 200 LeetCode problems is the most dangerous stage.  
  
Not 0, not 50, not even 500, 200 is the danger zone.  
  
At ~200 problems, you’ve seen enough to feel confident. You know arrays, binary search, basic DP, maybe a bit of graphs. You can solve most easy problems and some medium problems. And that’s exactly the problem.  
  
You start believing you “know DSA”. *You start skipping dry runs.* You start jumping to solutions faster than understanding them. *This is where many people stop improving.*  
  
At 200, you rely heavily on patterns you half-understand. You recognize problems but can’t derive solutions from scratch. You panic the moment the problem looks slightly different.  
  
*False confidence is more dangerous than no confidence.* When you’re at 0, you’re humble. When you’re at 200, you think you’re done. You’re not.  
  
Real growth starts when problems stop feeling familiar, and you’re forced to think again. That usually happens around 400–500 good-quality problems, not random ones.  
  
Go slow.  
Go deep.  
*And don’t confuse exposure with mastery.*  
  
Remember, divide these 500 questions per topic to be a master. You should solve 70-80 problems in trees, graphs, and DP each. And then 70-80 on arrays-strings & hashmap and heap, and then divide the rest among other topics
